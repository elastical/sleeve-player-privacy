About the app

Sleeve Player is a local music player for iOS. It does not provide or stream music content. You play files you already have. No account is required.

Adding music

Use the system file or folder picker to choose music from Files, iCloud Drive, or other file providers. The app keeps security-scoped bookmarks so it can continue accessing the folders or files you authorize.

Folders and access

Manage authorized folders in the app’s music folder management. Removing a folder’s access does not delete the original files. You can also set excluded directories so certain folders are skipped when scanning.

Lyrics

The app shows lyrics from matching-name .lrc files or lyrics embedded in audio tags. There is no online lyric search. You can adjust the lyrics offset in the app.

iCloud and file providers

Music stored in iCloud Drive or another file provider may need to download first. That download is handled by iOS or the file provider, according to your settings.

Playback

Background playback is supported. Lock Screen and Control Center controls show the current track and let you manage playback.

Data and privacy

Library and playback data stay on your device. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device. For privacy details, see the Privacy Policy.
