SLEEVE PLAYER · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your music stays on your device. Your privacy should be just as straightforward.

Updated and effective: 2026-10-28

No account required. The developer does not collect or upload your music library or listening history through the app.

Scope

This policy applies to the iOS version of Sleeve Player (折页音乐), a local music player provided by Sleeve Player Developer. It does not cover the Android version or third-party services.

Information processed on your device

To play and organize your music, the app reads or stores the following information on your device:

  • Audio files you select, file names and paths, track titles, artists, albums, duration, artwork and audio properties, for scanning, display and playback.
  • Local LRC files and lyrics embedded in audio tags, for lyric display and synchronization. Online lyric search is not included.
  • Playlists, favorites, queues, playback position, play counts, last-played times and recent play history, for playback restoration and smart lists.
  • Appearance, audio effects, sleep timer, lyric offsets, excluded folders, folder-access bookmarks, library indexes and audio-analysis caches.

The developer does not upload this information through the app to developer-operated servers or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Playback statistics are a local app feature, not analytics sent to the developer.

File access and system services

The app accesses the files or folders you select through the system picker and saves security-scoped bookmarks for continued access. You can remove authorized folders in the app’s music-folder settings. Removing a folder authorization does not automatically delete its original files.

Background audio allows playback to continue. The app supplies the current track metadata, artwork and playback progress to system Now Playing, Lock Screen and media controls. It does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

Networking, sharing and third parties

The current iOS version has no online lyric requests, developer-operated cloud library, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not automatically send your library or listening history to the developer or advertising services.

If you choose music stored in iCloud Drive or another file provider, iOS or that provider may download or synchronize files according to your settings and the provider’s policies. Device backups may include app data. This policy does not mean that system services never use the network.

When you choose to share a file, it is passed to the receiving app or service you select. Using the app’s file-deletion feature may delete the original audio file you select; review your choice before deleting.

Retention, deletion and control

Settings, indexes, caches, playlists and listening history are stored in the app’s data area on your device to support continued use. When a new play is recorded, recent history is trimmed to the preceding 90 days and a maximum of 200 entries per track. Total play counts and last-played times remain stored.

You can edit playlists, manage folders and change preferences in the app. Choosing “Delete App” removes the app and its local data from the device; “Offload App” retains documents and data. Music in external folders, shared copies and iCloud or other backups are not all deleted by removing the app; manage those separately with the relevant service.

The developer cannot remotely access or delete library and playback data that exist only on your device.

Information you send for support

If you choose to email the developer, your email address, message and any screenshots or diagnostic information you volunteer are used to respond, investigate the issue or handle your privacy request. Do not send unnecessary personal information or music files. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and necessary follow-up. You may request its deletion, except where retention is legally required.

Visiting this policy website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It embeds no advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts or developer-set tracking cookies. No sign-in is needed to read it.

GitHub, as the hosting provider, processes necessary access data such as IP addresses under its own policies. This is separate from music data processed by the app on your device. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect children’s personal information through account registration or analytics, and is not intended to gather information from children. Contact the developer if you believe support information you voluntarily sent includes a child’s personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when app features or data practices change, with the date shown on this page. Changes requiring notice or consent will be communicated appropriately as required by law.
